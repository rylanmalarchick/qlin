C6b, amplified ranking test on ibm_kingston: analysis plan, fixed before
any job runs
========================================================================
Written 2026-10-14, committed before the run.

Question: does five_qubit_code best_defer gain time per copy over source
on ibm_kingston, at the rate the block model predicts?

Design: each program runs with its body repeated k times in one circuit,
k in {1, 2, 4, 8}, every qubit reset between copies
(bench/run_phase4_ibm.py --copies). A copy's gap adds once per copy, while
the job-to-job noise (dominated by the repetition delay and queue state)
does not grow with k. So the slope of per-shot time against k measures the
gap per copy.
  Command: uv run --project bench python bench/run_phase4_ibm.py
    --bench five_qubit_code --variants source,best_defer --copies 1,2,4,8
    --repeats 15 --interleave --budget 300 --out phase7_c6b
  One round runs 8 jobs of 1000 shots in the order source_x1,
  best_defer_x1, source_x2, ..., best_defer_x8. One 100-shot smoke job
  first. If the budget cap stops the run early, the analysis uses the
  complete rounds.

Model prediction (block model, HERON_KINGSTON with the FakeKingston
snapshot, t_ff 600, t_branch 3450 ns = the measured branch cost; k = 1
exact, k > 1 sampled with 2000 records): gap per k 19.29, 38.51, 76.96,
153.84 us. Gap slope 19.22 us per copy.

Outlier rule (fixed now): drop a job whose per-shot time exceeds the
median of its (variant, k) cell by more than 1000 us. Report how many.

Primary analysis (bench/analyze_c6b.py):
  For each round r: least-squares slope of per-shot time on k for each
  variant (kept jobs, at least 2 k values each), and
  g_r = slope_source - slope_defer.
  1. Mean of g_r with a 95% t interval.
  2. Two-sided one-sample t test of g_r against 19.22 us.
  3. Wilcoxon signed-rank test of g_r against 0.
Reading:
  - The model ranking is confirmed if the interval in 1 lies above 0.
  - The model's size is rejected if test 2 gives p < 0.05.
  - Otherwise "not resolved".
Shapiro-Wilk on g_r is reported, as a check on the t tests.
